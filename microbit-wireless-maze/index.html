    How to connect a micro:bit v2
    1. Connect your micro:bit v2 with a USB data cable. Copy the Javalab MBL v6 firmware to the MICROBIT drive.
    2. Turn on Bluetooth and open this page in Chrome or Edge over HTTPS or on localhost. Disconnect the micro:bit from any other browser tabs.
    3. Select Connect micro:bit and choose your device. When the X, Y, and Z readings appear, lay the board flat with the LEDs facing up and select Calibrate.
