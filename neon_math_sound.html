How does the sound work?

The faint line previews the full shape; the bright line shows the path already traced. The moving dot’s vertical position controls a sine tone from 100 Hz at the bottom to 1,600 Hz at the top. The centre is 400 Hz. Equal vertical steps change pitch by equal musical intervals. This is a way to turn a graph into sound, not a recording of the waveform.

All angles use radians. Cartesian graphs keep x from −10 to 10 and y from −8 to 8 on every screen. Polar graphs use equal scales on both axes; their viewing range is shown on the graph. Points outside the viewing range and gaps at undefined values are silent.
