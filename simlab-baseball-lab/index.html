YOUR CHALLENGE

Can you hit the ball?

Build a curved ramp. Make it tilt with an Axis Connector. Add a motor to the bat, then use two keys to play.

  1. Get ready. Open Tinkercad with your class account. Use a computer with a keyboard for the number-key controls.
  2. Choose your starting point. Build the shapes with the video, or open the author’s tutorial model and make your own editable copy if copying is available.
  3. Follow the pictures. Keep this guide beside Tinkercad. Check one step, then move on.

Need help copying a model? Ask your teacher. The article explains the connectors in detail; the video shows the starting shape construction.
