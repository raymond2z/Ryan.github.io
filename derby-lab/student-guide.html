How to use Derby Lab

You have one car and two tracks. Can you change one thing and explain what happens?

1. Try a race

  1. Open Race Lab. Under Track profile, pick Straight slope.
  2. Start the race. Read the Estimated finish time.
  3. Predict what will happen if the wheels meet more resistance. Move only Rolling resistance towards Rough. Run the race again. Which time is longer?
Try Race Lab

2. Help the car clear a bend

  1. Open Balance Lab and choose Original track. Play the model. Watch the front of the car as the track bends.
  2. If the car stops on the track, choose Inspect chassis contact when it appears. The red dot shows where the car touched.
  3. In Direct car design, move Ground clearance. The blue arrow shows the space between flat ground and the bottom of your car. Keep the other sliders still and try again.
  4. After a finish, choose Inspect tightest gap. The blue mark shows where the car came closest to the track. How much space was left?
Try Balance Lab

3. Tell the story of your test

Open My experiment notebook. Enter your class and group code, choose the lab you used, and write your prediction. Choose Run & record experiment, then add what you noticed.

Try this sentence: “I changed ___ from ___ to ___. I kept ___ the same. My result was ___.”
